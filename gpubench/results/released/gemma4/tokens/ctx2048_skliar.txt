Let's think step by step.
Let $C$ be the number of chickens and $W$ be the number of cows.
The total number of animals is $C + W = 20$.
The total number of legs is $2C + 4W = 70$.
From the first equation, we can express $W$ as $W = 20 - C$.
Substitute this into the second equation:
$2C + 4(20 - C) = 70$
$2C + 80 - 4C = 70$
$-2C + 80 = 70$
$-2C = 70 - 80$
