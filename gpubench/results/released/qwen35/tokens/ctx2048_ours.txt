Let's assume all 20 animals are chickens first.
If all 20 were chickens, the total number of legs would be 20 * 2 = 40 legs.
The actual total number of legs is 70.
The difference between the actual legs and the assumed legs is 70 - 40 = 30 extra legs.
Each cow has 2 more legs than a chicken (4 legs vs 2 legs).
So, the number of cows is 30 / 2 = 15 cows.
Since the total number of animals is 20, the number of chickens is 20 - 15 = 5 chickens.
