Let $c$ be the number of chickens and $o$ be the number of cows.
We know that chickens have 2 legs and cows have 4 legs.
We have two equations:
1. $c + o = 20$ (total animals)
2. $2c + 4o = 70$ (total legs)

From equation 1, we can express $o$ in terms of $c$: $o = 20 - c$.
Substitute this into equation 2:
$2c + 4(20 - c) = 70$
$2c + 80 - 4c = 70$
$-2c + 80 = 70$
$-2c
